# FactionOS

### Advanced TORN Faction Management

FactionOS is an independent Discord bot for TORN faction management.

## Features

- Automatic Payout Calculations (PPH, PPR & Hybrid)
- Personalized Gym Stats Plans
- Live War Dashboards & Chain Tracking
- Organized Crime Management
- Faction Banking
- Member Verification
- Stock Tracking
- TORN, FFScouter & Weav3r Integration
